Delete your Thornmarked account

For the Thornmarked app (package com.minormending.thornmarked), by Standard Mending Co. An account exists only if you chose to sign in with Google to keep your saves on other devices.

In the app

Open the You tab, go to Your story on other devices, and tap Delete account and cloud saves. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email kkramdath@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Thornmarked account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

Your email address, your Google account ID, your cloud saves and your sign-in sessions are removed from our server. We don't keep any of it afterwards.

Saves on your phone are not affected; to remove them, use "Reset this save" on the You tab or uninstall the app.
