Thornmarked privacy policy

For the Thornmarked app (package com.minormending.thornmarked), version 1.0.0 and later. Last updated: November 9, 2026.

Thornmarked is a single-player interactive romance story for readers 17 and over. You don't need an account to play, and the app has no advertising, analytics or tracking. It uses the internet for two things: downloading new story chapters and art, and, from version 1.6.1, downloading updates to the app itself, so both can arrive without a Play Store update. The story you already have keeps working without a connection.

Story downloads

When you open the app while online, it asks our content server for the latest published story and for any art it needs. The server runs on Cloudflare, which hosts it for us. Each request is a plain HTTPS download that carries nothing about you, your choices or your save. Like any internet request it reveals your device's IP address and the standard information a phone sends with a web request. Cloudflare may keep that request information briefly in its logs as our service provider, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't use it to identify players.

App updates

From version 1.6.1, when the app starts it checks Expo's update service (expo.dev), which delivers updates to the app's code and screens. That request carries technical details needed to deliver the update, such as the app version, platform and update channel, plus a random install identifier that the update service creates the first time the app runs. The identifier is not linked to you, your account or your device's hardware; it lets the service deliver the right update and count how many installs run each version. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. The request does not include your name, contacts, location, your save or anything you do in the story. Expo's own privacy policy is at expo.dev/privacy.

What stays on your device

Your saves, your choices and the story so far are kept in the app's local storage on your device and are never sent anywhere. So are your preferences: who you'd like to court, how spicy you like the romance scenes, content notes and your character's look.

To confirm you are 17 or over, the app asks for your date of birth once. It checks your age on the device and keeps only your birth year, also on the device. Your date of birth is never sent anywhere.

The app turns off Android backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account. A new phone starts with a fresh save.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app checks for a new chapter in the background every few hours, using the same story download described above. When one is ready, your device shows a notification. The app doesn't register with a push notification service or send any identifier for notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in Android settings.

What we do not collect

Thornmarked does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, hardware device identifiers, advertising ID or any other personal information. It contains no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or crash reporting. The only data that leaves your device is the story download and the app update check described above; the update check's random install identifier is the only identifier involved.

Purchases

Version 1.0.0 has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added later, Google Play will handle the payment, and this policy will be updated before then.

Children

Thornmarked is not for children. It is rated for players 17 and over, it asks for a date of birth to keep younger players out, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices and deletion

To delete your saves and preferences, use "Reset this save" on the You tab, clear Thornmarked's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. We don't keep any account or server copy of your game, so there is nothing else to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
